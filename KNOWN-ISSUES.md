# Known issues and open items

This guide is a draft published before its final read-through. The items below are open. Items fixed in this revision are listed at the end.

## Open

1. **Brand and trademark review is not finished.** ISTQB® marks and wording are still under review. Do not treat the guide as final until this review is complete.
2. **Final independent read-through is not done.** The guide is not yet marked final.
3. **Content review is in progress.** Findings from external reviews are being checked one by one. Not all of them are accepted.
4. **Site search is not available.** The site is static HTML. To search, open `all.html` and use the browser's find function.
5. **Only the HTML edition is published.** PDF and EPUB editions are paused.
6. **Exam-condition facts need checking before final publication.** Format, timing, extra-time rule and pass mark should be re-checked against the official ISTQB® CTFL page. The extra-time rule (25% when the candidate's native language is not the exam language) comes from Exam Structures and Rules v1.2. How it is applied depends on the exam provider and the Member Board.
7. **Other editions are separate works.** The Turkish edition and its mock exams will be published separately, after their own review.

## Fixed in this revision

- The Leanpub publisher line has been removed from the copyright page, the title block and the full-text page.
- A feedback contact has been added to the copyright page and the README.
- A chapter 4 user-story sentence that was too close to the syllabus wording has been rewritten.
- An uncited statement about bilingual exams not granting extra time has been removed.
- The wording about per-LO question counts now matches the official Exam Structure Tables.
- The 2-value boundary value analysis note now refers to the nearest neighbour across each boundary, not only to invalid partitions.
- Passages that stayed too close to the syllabus wording have been rewritten in our own words across chapters 1–6, the front and back matter, and the Mock Exam 1 explanations.

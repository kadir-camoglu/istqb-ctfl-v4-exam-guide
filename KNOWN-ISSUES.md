# Known issues and open items (draft)

This draft is published before the final read-through. The items below are known gaps or inconsistencies. They will be fixed before the guide is marked final.

## Content and publishing

1. **Leanpub reference.** The copyright page and the full-text page say "Published via Leanpub." This line is to be removed in the final publishing step.
2. **Brand and trademark review is not finished.** ISTQB® marks and wording are being reviewed. The site must not be presented as final until that review is complete.
3. **Final independent read-through is not done.** The guide is not yet marked `status: final`.
4. **Mock exam 1 overlaps with another practice exam.** A question-by-question comparison found that all 40 questions test the same learning objectives, and 38 are reworded versions of the same questions. Decision pending: revise the mock with new questions or keep it as is.
5. **Site search is missing.** The site is static HTML, so there is no full-text search yet. Use the browser's find function on `all.html` in the meantime.
6. **Only the HTML edition is published.** PDF and EPUB versions are paused and are not part of this site.
7. **Turkish edition and Turkish mock exams are not published.** They will follow after this English guide is finalized.

## Exam conditions

- The exam format, timing and pass mark can change. The guide links to the official ISTQB® CTFL page and asks readers to check their national board or exam provider. Check that the links still work before final publication.
- The "+25% extension" rule depends on the provider. The guide explains this, but the exact rule should be re-checked before final publication.
